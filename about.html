About Ether's Magnetric Universe

Ether's Magnetric Universe is an interactive journey inspired by Magnetric Being by Anthony Tyrel Parker (Ether).

Explore 18 worlds, each named for a chapter. Begin at the outermost orbit and work inward through self-guided reflections. At the center, the Magnetric Sun represents the integration of Spirit, Soul, Mind, and Body.
